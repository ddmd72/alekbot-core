    fields: "Each fact has a number n, its text, domain, temporal_class, tags, metadata, context, reported_date and created_at (local days in the owner's timezone). Dates in the text, tags and metadata are content. A fact whose reported_date is null cannot take a horizon."

    answer: "Exactly one decision per fact, by its number n: kind (date, horizon or none); date (YYYY-MM-DD, with kind date only: the last day the fact holds); days (a whole number from 1 to 36500, with kind horizon only); unsure (true or false); reason (one short line in English: the kinds_table row that applies and the words of the fact that decide it)."
